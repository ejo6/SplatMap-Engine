# Landing page `/` (K-11)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Hack Western judges on the demo laptop / Devpost link (PRODUCT.md). Action: paste a YouTube link → `/w/<id>`, or open a demo world. Proof: the mechanism (video → walkable world, fog where unfilmed), gallery of real worlds when baked, extension install. Constraints: light page (no 3D), offline-capable assets, creator credit, no fabricated imagery or claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A video is a reflection; we let you step out of it. The page is a bright mountain lake where the waterline is the threshold between the flat video (the reflection) and the place itself (the land above). Refuses the dark SaaS hero with a screenshot and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Daylight alpine. Moraine Lake at 6AM photograph (Unsplash, Redd Francisco) as the ground; sky blue, glacier turquoise, spruce green, snow white; deep spruce-navy ink; one YouTube red for the single action. Characterful variable grotesk display, set big in the sky. Waterline as a horizontal rule that recurs as the section divider; trails and ridgelines as line work.

STORY: In one viewport the judge reads the one-liner, sees the reflection ripple under the pointer, and pastes a link on the waterline. Scrolling: the worlds gallery, how it works as a three-waypoint trail, the fog principle (we never invent what wasn't filmed), the extension, credits.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photo; headline "Every video is a window." huge in the sky, upper left; the link field sits exactly on the lake's waterline; "We turn it into a door." below it over the reflection; slim header in the sky; photo credit bottom right.

FORM: Pinned by the user, so no roll position. seed: none (pinned) "I would like something very bright and nature oriented … this is the angle we want to go with" plus the confirmed waterline concept. Signature interaction: the pointer ripples the reflection; a valid link projects that video's thumbnail onto the water, and submit rises through the waterline into the peaks.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
